# CHR-A-01 — The Father's Wisdom: Desmond Doss

**Status:** Premise approved; verification and scripting pending. **Format:** Recurring father-son opening → historical dramatized cutaway → grounded return.

**Viewer:** A father who wants a son to understand earned courage and conviction; wants to model character instead of merely saying the right words.

**Father-son spark:** The son wonders whether a soldier who refused to carry a gun could possibly be brave. Father treats the objection seriously.

**Independent cutaway:** Desmond Doss, US Army medic and conscientious objector, saved wounded soldiers under fire in Okinawa, 1945. The cutaway is an actual historical story, not an animation of the son's original question.

**Emotional turn:** The son revises his idea of bravery; the father demonstrates wisdom through example and conversation, not a polished sermon. Return to an ordinary shared task/setting for authentic human texture.

**Official primary source lead:** https://www.army.mil/medalofhonor/citations20.html (search “DOSS, DESMOND T.”); secondary: https://desmonddoss.org/medal-of-honor/

**Accuracy:** Check historical actions, conscientious-objector classification and numbers before filming. Distinguish dramatization from verified quotes. Do not use dialogue from *Hacksaw Ridge* as historical source.

**Assets:** Reusable father/son garage or porch, historical medic illustration, battlefield silhouettes and rescue; limited respectful motion and sound.

**Unvalidated digital continuation:** Father-son virtue lessons/activity pack.

**Second execution:** A different father-son problem illustrated by a distinct biblical or historical example, TBD.
