# Account mini-bible: Christian Character & Virtue

**Status:** Two test formats. Denominational alignment, precise buyers and offers are unvalidated.

**Emotional universe:** Faith, fatherhood, courage, duty, sacrifice, moral authority, traditions and the desire to be a man children respect. Also nostalgia, grief, perceived hypocrisy and frustration with cultural direction. Do not turn these into invented factual scapegoats.

**Separate buyer hypotheses:** Christian fathers seeking practical ways to pass on character and faith; Christian men seeking their own moral/spiritual formation. Respect Catholic/Protestant differences where relevant.

**Format A — The Father's Wisdom:** A grounded father-son scene; son asks or encounters something important; father's response opens a genuinely *different* illustrative historical, biblical, or parable cutaway; then return for an earned resolution. The father is strong and wise but recognizably human. First: Desmond Doss and the meaning of courage.

**Format B — The Price of Conviction:** Standalone researched drama around a real historical Christian moral choice and its consequences. First: Maximilian Kolbe at Auschwitz. Historical suffering is not sensational entertainment; treat sources and victims respectfully.

**Styles:** A grounded everyday adult cartoon (King of the Hill / F Is for Family *reference only*); B mature serious dramatic cel-shaded series (Young Justice/Invincible design vocabulary). Modern framing and historical cutaways should be coherent visually, with cinematic changes mainly from staging/lighting.

**Unvalidated digital products:** Father-son discussion/activity guides, character exercises, documented history and reflections; expertise credibility must be earned.

**Pilot briefs:** `pilot-A-the-fathers-wisdom.md` and `pilot-B-the-price-of-conviction.md`.
