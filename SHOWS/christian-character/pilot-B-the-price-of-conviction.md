# CHR-B-01 — The Price of Conviction: Maximilian Kolbe

**Status:** Premise approved; verify source detail and write script. **Format:** Independent historical moral dilemma.

**Viewer desire:** Courage, conviction and sacrifice with a real price, not empty motivational performance.

**Promise:** A Franciscan priest held at Auschwitz volunteers to take the place of another prisoner condemned to die.

**Story spine:** Nazi camp reprisal → prisoners selected to be starved → Maximilian Kolbe volunteers to substitute for Franciszek Gajowniczek → substitution accepted → Kolbe killed after time in the starvation cell, and the other man survives.

**Primary historical sources:** Auschwitz-Birkenau State Museum: https://www.auschwitz.org/en/museum/news/64th-anniversary-of-the-death-of-st-maksymilian-kolbe-in-auschwitz%2C425.html and https://www.auschwitz.org/en/history/punishments-and-executions/starvation-to-death

**Non-negotiable:** Documentary seriousness about the Holocaust. No fabricated survivor or perpetrator dialogue, invented last words, invented camera angles posing as archival footage, sensational gore, or unsupported chronology. Clearly identify dramatizations as illustrations.

**Opening direction:** Present the consequential human choice first, then supply necessary historical orientation. Don't preach before the dramatic situation is clear.

**Assets:** Restrained historical camp architecture, character silhouettes, dates/captions, spare sound, limited character animation.

**Unvalidated offer:** Documented Christian history and reflection case collection.

**Second execution:** A distinct, sourced Christian-conviction story, TBD.
