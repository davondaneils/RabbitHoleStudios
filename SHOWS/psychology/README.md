# Account mini-bible: Psychology, Power & Influence

**Status:** Two pilot hypotheses. Buyer, final name and paid offer unvalidated.

**Audience desire:** See the hidden game. Spot deception, avoid humiliation and being fooled; gain influence and social competence. Emotional palette includes curiosity, status, resentment, control, independence and pleasure in an intelligent reversal.

**Do not conflate two potential buyers:** Fans of real-world scams/cognitive biases versus people who pay for negotiation, communication or persuasion training.

**Format A — The Setup:** Reconstruct sourced cases of manufactured trust, fake social proof, authority and deception as dramatic investigations. First case: The Shed at Dulwich.

**Format B — The Human Experiment:** Put the viewer inside a decision, then reveal a researched psychological mechanism and caveats. First: subscription decoy effect.

**The Setup visual direction (selected 2026-10-13):** Polished 2D TV-animation aesthetic with mature, original adult character designs, clean crisp linework, controlled cel shading, believable expressive faces and cinematic framing. Closely approach the visual sophistication of Archer as a broad aesthetic reference, without copying its characters, distinctive designs or frames. This supersedes the briefly considered lower-complexity/simplified treatment for The Setup. Production repeatability/cost still needs testing. The previously proposed simplified expressive educational style remains a separate comparison hypothesis, not the chosen look for The Setup.

**The Setup character-generation source of truth:** [`the-setup-style-guide.md`](the-setup-style-guide.md) → [`the-setup-base-prompt.md`](the-setup-base-prompt.md) + [`the-setup-negative-prompt.md`](the-setup-negative-prompt.md); see episode-specific dossiers such as [`oobah-butler-character-dossier.md`](oobah-butler-character-dossier.md). Match the **simple bold contours/flat cel shading** from supplied TV reference frames, not excessive illustration detail. Actual real-person photos establish likeness. “Much better” is candidate feedback, not approval. Higgsfield generations do not override written decisions.

**Voice:** Specific, sharp, sometimes amused. No fake dialogue attributed to real people, generic dark-psychology hype or universal behavioral claims.

**Unvalidated digital offers:** Interactive scam spotting; negotiation exercises; psychological decision cases; a deeper sourced case library. Validate buyer overlap.

**Pilot briefs:** `pilot-A-the-setup.md` and `pilot-B-the-human-experiment.md`.
