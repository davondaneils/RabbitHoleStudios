# Account mini-bible: Psychology, Power & Influence

**Status:** Two pilot hypotheses. Buyer, final name and paid offer unvalidated.

**Audience desire:** See the hidden game. Spot deception, avoid humiliation and being fooled; gain influence and social competence. Emotional palette includes curiosity, status, resentment, control, independence and pleasure in an intelligent reversal.

**Do not conflate two potential buyers:** Fans of real-world scams/cognitive biases versus people who pay for negotiation, communication or persuasion training.

**Format A — The Setup:** Reconstruct sourced cases of manufactured trust, fake social proof, authority and deception as dramatic investigations. First case: The Shed at Dulwich.

**Format B — The Human Experiment:** Put the viewer inside a decision, then reveal a researched psychological mechanism and caveats. First: subscription decoy effect.

**Styles under test:** A mature, original, adult spy/crime cel-shaded cartoon (Archer-like vocabulary, not character imitation); B simplified expressive educational cartoon (OverSimplified-like vocabulary).

**Voice:** Specific, sharp, sometimes amused. No fake dialogue attributed to real people, generic dark-psychology hype or universal behavioral claims.

**Unvalidated digital offers:** Interactive scam spotting; negotiation exercises; psychological decision cases; a deeper sourced case library. Validate buyer overlap.

**Pilot briefs:** `pilot-A-the-setup.md` and `pilot-B-the-human-experiment.md`.
