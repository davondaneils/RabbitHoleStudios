# The Setup — anti-drift / negative prompt (v1)
Paste alongside the [base prompt](the-setup-base-prompt.md) for each character sheet. Keep short and directly relevant; more words do not replace actual images.

## Copy/paste negative block
> NO soft color gradients; NO painterly brushwork; NO photorealistic rendering; NO 3D/Pixar lighting; NO realistic skin texture or pores; NO smooth airbrushed cheek shadows; NO hatch marks, stippling or tiny wrinkles; NO individual hair strands or elaborate hair highlights; NO intricate facial microdetail; NO glossy comic-book / concept-art finish; NO over-rendered lighting; NO exaggerated anime or children's-cartoon proportions.
>
> NO square-jawed generic action hero, default handsome spy face, heroic brow, perfectly symmetrical facial structure, borrowed face or haircut from any existing Archer character, suit-by-default archetype, or flattering changes that erase the real person's identity.
>
> NO invented extra characters, recurring visible host, mascot, animal-headed presenter, dramatic background scene, props, art-direction board full of paragraphs, duplicate front views instead of actual 3/4 and side profiles, or changes of haircut/outfit/face between views.

## What the negative block is guarding against
- **Too much detail** was the repeated actual failure. The supplied TV examples use *fewer* bold, carefully placed marks, not hyper-detailed drawn wrinkles and individual hair strands.
- **Face contamination:** Earlier generations used the identity of the Archer screenshots (square-jawed, model-like, styled hair) rather than using only their graphic technique. The real photo wins for identity.
- **Inconsistent turnarounds:** All views must be of one coherent individual; actual 3/4, profile, and varied expressions, not duplicated or posterized front views.
- **Unverifiable promises:** A strong prompt isn't proof. Visually compare the output to references, reject/adjust, record the actual model and outcome.

**Positive rules still lead.** If negatives make the model ignore the real person's likeness or adult TV look, shorten them and emphasize the specific real photo + style-image distinction.
