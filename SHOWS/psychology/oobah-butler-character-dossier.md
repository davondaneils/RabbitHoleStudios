## Identity details to PRESERVE from actual photos
- **Hair:** pale/light blond, distinctive naturally uneven **forward-falling fringe**, soft tousled/slightly longer top, not a short neat side-part or dark/blond slicked Archer haircut. Capture actual era-specific length and hairline from photos rather than imposing a fixed haircut across his whole lifetime.
- **Face:** softer/rounder-oval face rather than dramatic chiseled spy/superhero jaw; real nose, eyes, brows, cheek fullness, mouth and chin should follow identity photos. Light brows/eyes visible in photos. Preserve natural asymmetry rather than heroic symmetry.
- **Presentation:** recognizable, ordinary, wry British journalist/prankster; not an anonymous square-jawed suit model.
- **Working screen wardrobe (NOT independently confirmed historical outfit):** dark charcoal/black casual jacket, plain dark or light crew-neck top, dark trousers, simple sneakers. Keep one outfit consistent across the sheet; verify scene-specific clothes when historical accuracy matters.
