# SUR-A-01 — The Decision: Flooded road

**Status:** Premise approved; safety fact-check needed before script. **Format:** Viewer makes one realistic choice, then learns the safe response.

**Viewer desire:** Stay calm and capable when family needs a consequential decision.

**Scene:** Heavy rain at night; a family is driving home. Floodwater covers the road. The alternate route adds time. A passenger asks whether it's really that deep. Give the viewer a choice without making water depth appear reliably visible.

**Payoff:** Never drive into floodwater. Water can hide damaged roads and be powerful even when apparently shallow; turn around and find a safe route. Location-specific safe actions and evacuation instructions must be verified.

**Official research lead:** US National Weather Service “Turn Around Don't Drown”: https://www.weather.gov/safety/flood-turn-around-dont-drown

**Animation assets:** Vehicle silhouette, headlights, water shape/flow, route map, a small number of choices, readable reveal. Works with either Survival style.

**Caveat:** Fictional illustration, not real footage. No invented hydrology, bogus safe-depth thresholds or risky water-crossing advice.

**Unvalidated offer:** Emergency choice drills and preparedness planners.
