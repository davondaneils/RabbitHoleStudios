# Account mini-bible: Survival & Preparedness

**Status:** Two pilot hypotheses; buyer and product unvalidated.

**Core desire:** “If systems failed, could I protect the people who depend on me?” Fear and uncertainty paired with competence, foresight, responsibility and pride in being dependable. The opponent is usually a real physical constraint, bad assumption, missing resource or cascading failure.

**Format A — The Decision:** One concrete emergency and a consequential viewer choice. Give an honest, evidence-backed outcome, not fearmongering. First: flooded road at night. Never suggest driving through floodwater.

**Format B — The Breaking Point:** Recurring family and community facing compounding infrastructure failures. Choices carry into later installments, while every episode resolves a local question. First: floor-20 apartment power/water failure and neighbor needing help.

**Styles:** A mostly-white, very minimal line/stick-figure animated diagram; B original flat, expressive adult-cartoon cast with recurring personalities (South Park-like economy, not its characters).

**Voice:** Specific time, objects, terrain, alternatives and safety constraints. Verify safety recommendations with credible current sources.

**Unvalidated digital products:** Household emergency plan, preparedness checklist, scenario-based family drills, interactive readiness audit.

**Pilot briefs:** `pilot-A-the-decision.md`, `pilot-B-the-breaking-point.md`.
