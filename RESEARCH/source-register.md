## Claim register in each research dossier

**Claim** / **supporting link** / **what the source actually says** / **confidence** / **script wording** / **image/footage licensing note**.

- Primary sources first: official museums, agencies, original accounts, published research. Cross-check original accounts with independent reporting where possible.
- For psychological effects, distinguish replicated tendencies under conditions from deterministic 'tricks'.
- For documented real people, separate archival facts, plausible illustration and invented dramatic dialogue.
- For emergency content, safety rules are higher priority than suspense. Local emergency services and official guidance supersede fictional choices.
- Source permissions and copyright status matter for screenshots, historical photos, clips, audio and logos; linking is not licensing.
- This repo doesn't establish that sources are licensed for commercial use.

## Survival positioning research queue — 2026-10-15

**Origin:** Founder supplied a positioning handoff and approved updating the working direction. These are research tasks and leads, not completed customer research or validated offers. Use the [survival mini-bible](../SHOWS/survival/README.md) for current decisions.

1. **Choose a first context:** Geography, housing type, household responsibilities, budget and existing readiness. The initial segment hypothesis is a household decision-maker who feels underprepared and needs priorities. Do not inherit a US-first, homeowner-only or battery-buyer assumption.
2. **Find the real application problem:** Collect authentic audience language about what to prepare, conflicting priorities, household coordination and applying generic advice. Distinguish quoted customers from synthetic hypotheses. Test whether people want planning help, equipment selection, or merely interesting disruption stories.
3. **Free baseline:** [Public Safety Canada outage guidance](https://www.canada.ca/en/services/policing/emergencies/preparedness/get-prepared/hazards-emergencies/power-outages/how-prepare.html) was opened during the handoff assessment. Select equivalent current local sources after choosing geography. Check what a paid planning product would help buyers apply beyond available free guidance. Linking a Canadian source is not a decision to target Canada.
4. **Energy context:** [EIA reserves explanation](https://www.eia.gov/tools/faqs/faq.php?id=38&t=6) and the [dated IEA October 2026 report](https://www.iea.org/reports/oil-market-report-october-2026) were opened during assessment. Do not turn reserves ratios into exhaustion deadlines or oil-market disruption into a local blackout prediction. Reverify any dated claim when used.
5. **Commercial research leads from the handoff:** [The Prepared](https://theprepared.com/), [City Prepping](https://cityprepping.com/membership-sign-up/), [Will Prowse](https://willapproved.com/), [DIY Solar Forum](https://diysolarforum.com/) and [HOBOTECH](https://hobotech.tv/). These pages/offers were not investigated as part of this update. Study needs, language and offer structure without adopting technical claims or inferring paid demand from popularity. [4Patriots sales page](https://go.4patriots.com/ppg-1200/) is a proposed sales-structure example only; its claims and economics require independent assessment.

**Decision needed after research:** First audience/geography and one practical blackout episode promise. Product differentiation, scope/expert review, pricing, maintenance and acquisition economics remain unresolved. No product-building or paid testing is authorized by this research queue.
