# Weekly operating system

## Stage

**Current: setup / pilot production.** Founder time is capped at eight focused hours per week because web-design work remains first priority. Account creation, digital products, memberships, contractors, and extra subscriptions only when they earn their place.

## Two lead measures for this stage

1. **Approved videos actually published** (not drafts or script count).
2. **Founder minutes per approved published video** (production-system leverage, not a vanity efficiency number).

Quality, story engagement and credible source research are acceptance constraints, not optional tradeoffs. Watch time/retention, shares and follows are outcome signals. When selling, qualified site visits and actual purchases matter more than views alone.

## Weekly ritual

- **15 minutes, commission:** choose next videos, define primary promise, check missing research.
- **Production blocks:** start one timer when doing focused work, pause when idle / waiting on remote renders. A timer per *block* is enough; do not force bookkeeping by shot.
- **Publish and log:** fill only the relevant columns of `TRACKING/pilots.csv`.
- **30-minute weekly review:** What got finished? Which hook/format got watch-through/follows/profile visits? What was surprisingly expensive? What to kill, modify or repeat next week?

Original ambition: 3 accounts, 2 formats each, 2 executions each = 12 pilot videos. With early setup, **6-9 total/week is an aspiration, not guaranteed**; 9 then 12 per week become real lead targets only after production time proves it. Don't make junk to meet an invented quota.

## Standard minimal fields

ID, account, format, story, visual direction, status, publish link/date, founder minutes, tool spend, 24h views, hold/watch time, shares, follows, profile visits, click/purchase if available, next decision.

No precise conversion modeling before we observe real outbound traffic and purchases. A pilot 'wins' only with repeatable signal across multiple executions, not a single viral spike.

## Decisions and updates

- Final publishing/creative judgment: founder.
- AI: research, creative divergence, source audit, first scripts, shot plans and diagnostic edits.
- Later human editor / contractor: repeatable shot assembly after SOP and economics are demonstrated.
- Reuse assets; never clone the same script with different nouns. If founder work exceeds cap, simplify scenes, postpone marginal pilots or delegate *proven* mechanics, not change the cap by stealth.

At session end: update tracker status, next action in `README.md`, and decision log if needed. Do not rewrite the studio manual because of one noisy view count.
