# Decisions and open hypotheses

## LOCKED - 2026-10-12

- Business vehicle: portfolio of faceless original animated short-form shows linked to relevant digital products/experiences. This is a *commercial experiment*, not a promise of profitability.
- Web-design work is primary. Studio gets a **hard initial eight founder-hour weekly cap**.
- First accounts: **Psychology / Power / Influence**, **Survival / Preparedness**, **Christian Character / Virtue**.
- First two pilot formats per account and their first episode ideas are listed in `README.md` and their pilot briefs. Six first stories are approved to develop; NOT approved scripts or finished videos.
- Visual directions: Psychology A mature spy cartoon / B simplified educational cartoon; Survival A mostly white minimal stick figure / B flat expressive adult cartoon; Christian A grounded family cartoon / B serious cel-shaded drama. All are *competing style hypotheses*, not a commitment to fully producing all combinations.
- Storytelling: full emotional audience reality and shadow; specific credible antagonists as narrative conflicts; show-don't-tell; real-world specific details; authentic human voice/associative movement; holistic coherence; ruthless subtraction. This is a **palette**, not a mandatory checklist.
- Episode structure may be standalone, serial, interactive, documentary, historical dramatization, or character framing + cutaway. Multi-part long-form-to-Reels distribution remains a **testable option**, not a requirement.
- Work needs to be externalized into living Markdown docs; GitHub private repository is now the active source of truth.

## LOCKED - 2026-10-13 (The Setup visual workflow)

- **Chosen The Setup visual target:** original adult **2D TV cel animation** broadly inspired by the visual grammar of Archer: smooth bold black contours, sparse internal lines, matte flat color shapes, very few hard-edged angular shadows, credible individualized adult anatomy and understated facial performances. **Polish comes from controlled simplicity, not more rendering/detail.** Original characters; do not copy TV character faces or shots. This applies to all episodes and all real-person characters in The Setup.
- **Identity separation:** supplied TV screenshots determine **style only**, while real photographic reference(s), preferably relevant to the episode's time period, determine each person's **actual face and haircut**. The anthology has a distinct off-screen narrator, no recurring visible host/animal mascot, and uses the case-specific real people.
- **Repeatability:** the required style guide, base prompt, negative prompt, character dossiers and QA/approval states are maintained in `SHOWS/psychology/`. GitHub is the source of truth; Higgsfield is a generation workspace. Do not silently promote a new character candidate or model choice to approved status.

## HYPOTHESES / NOT LOCKED

- Exact ICP segmentation and buyer persona, willingness to pay, digital product offers, member pricing, and downstream economics.
- Final show titles/branding, narration voices, **individual approved character sheets**, story lengths, distribution cadence. The Oobah Seedream 5 Lite sheet is a **promising candidate** (“much better”), not yet formally approved.
- Final production software stack; actual cost per successful short; whether AI-generated video beats controllable illustrated motion for any given format. The successful Oobah candidate used Higgsfield Seedream 5 Lite, but that does **not** lock the tool/model.
- Whether the selected visuals produce different market response. Match creative claims, keep tests small.
- Whether three accounts remain viable. Kill or concentrate when evidence arrives.

## SUPERSEDED historical choices

- An older Creative OS illustrated Mystery, Survival and Transformation as the first three horses; that list is no longer the active first-round slate.
- Earlier opportunity atlas rankings and per-million-view earnings projections used speculative weights/conversion assumptions, NOT validated sales or financial forecasts. Do not treat them as data.
- Older 12-videos-every-week expectation was adjusted: it's a **stretch once the pipeline proves efficient**, not a guaranteed launch week quota.
- Earlier pressure to build brands, elaborate show universes, full courses, community, paid ads or automations before pilots was rejected.

## How to record future decisions
