# Narration and dialogue a scrolling viewer can follow
Sources: original Creative OS §§14–16; active `../creative-os.md`. Added founder requirement, 2026-10-14: Instagram narration must be immediately understandable, simple and connected.

**A distracted first-time listener should understand each sentence and its connection to the previous sentence without reconstructing the meaning.** Simplicity concerns the listener's work, not sentence length alone.

- Prefer familiar words, concrete verbs and a clear subject. Introduce people and things before referring to them indirectly.
- Use complete, connected sentences as the default. Give each sentence one main idea; closely related clauses can stay together when they explain cause and effect.
- Keep the useful connective words. “So strangers started calling to book tables” explains a consequence; several isolated nouns do not.
- Use a fragment only when its meaning is already clear and it adds a natural emphasis. A succession of fragments is not a substitute for rhythm.
- Say what happened plainly before adding interpretation. Avoid abstract labels, overloaded metaphors, nested flashbacks and clever lines that require decoding.
- Keep time shifts explicit. Distinguish a fictional viewer scenario from a historical person's documented actions or thoughts.
- Make narration sound like one person explaining an interesting story. Do not manufacture slang, repeated rhetorical questions, symmetrical lists or constant punchlines.

Example of repair: “A seventieth birthday. Nine people. Someone was asking four months ahead.” → “One caller wanted to book a seventieth-birthday dinner for nine people, four months in advance.” The relationship is explicit without making the audience assemble it.

Dialogue is optional. When justified, give characters clear goals and distinct behavior; do not use them solely to recite exposition. Never invent dialogue for real people and present it as documented.

Text pass: identify ambiguous references, missing links and unnatural breath units. Actual delivery pass: listen to the chosen voice and check pace, emphasis and comprehension. Never claim that a silent review constitutes a spoken test.

## Clarity across sound and visuals
Use the [multimedia/coherence evidence](../../RESEARCH/short-form-scriptwriting-2026-10-14.md) as a reason to test competition among speech, music, captions and images, not a blanket music ban. Audio alone should carry the basic who/what/why; visuals can supply relevant reveals without forcing the narrator to describe every pixel. Protect essential meaning and source qualifications when cutting. Actual one-listen understanding is tested through [testing.md](testing.md), not inferred from short sentences, readability scores or a model's approval.

Direct digital-reading research ([Berger, Moe & Schweidel, 2023](https://journals.sagepub.com/doi/full/10.1177/00222429231152880)) links processing ease with holding attention; its setting still differs from spoken Reels. Use familiar concrete wording and simple relationships, then test actual listening. The finding does not justify reducing every sentence to a fragment or treating a readability number as audience validation.
