# Critical editing: repair passages, not score compliance
Sources: active Creative OS eight-step process and final five questions; original Creative OS §§3–4, 14–19. Apply after a full draft.

Read once for the story before auditing individual lines. Then make a skeptical pass:

1. **Immediate understanding:** Mark the first place a newcomer might lose who, what, when or why. Replace vague references and restore missing connective language. Do not explain confusion away in production notes.
2. **Hook contract:** State what the opening promises and locate the payoff. Repair misleading emphasis or an answer that never arrives.
3. **Audience value:** Identify where the events carry the audience's concern. Remove instructions to care, generic psychology lectures and unearned buyer assumptions.
4. **Progression and emotion:** Each beat must alter the situation, evidence or expectation. Check that comedy, tension and quieter moments serve the same story.
5. **Natural voice:** Flag forced fragments, sterile essay language, unexplained abstractions, repetitive rhetorical structures and cleverness that delays comprehension. Replace with plain speech.
6. **Subtraction:** Cut repeated explanation, duplicate examples and extra endings. Preserve words that clarify relationships and facts that qualify a claim. Word count is information, not a quality score.
7. **Accuracy:** Check claims, quotations, imagined scenes and psychological interpretations against actual sources. Do not infer certainty from a polished narrative.
8. **Delivery and visuals:** Identify what still needs a real voice read, first-frame or sound-off test. Report whether each was actually performed.

Record only the highest-impact problems using **passage → listener problem → concrete repair**, plus material remaining weaknesses. A list of techniques present is not evidence of a good script. The author can make a separate critical pass but must not call it independent review.

Finish with the five questions: why stop; what happens and why care; what changes; does the ending repay the opening; what can be removed without making understanding worse? Revise actual weaknesses before presenting the candidate. Do not ask the founder to approve a framework instead of reading the finished script.

Preserve earlier candidates. Mark the new version as unapproved. Measured comprehension, retention and commercial results require evidence outside this editorial process.
