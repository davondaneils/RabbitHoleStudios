## Audience-value gate — explicit working answer
- **Viewer job / evidence level:** Hypothesized viewer wants to avoid being fooled and feel more discerning. No new audience interviews or validated buyer evidence are claimed.
- **Why this story:** People can appear to verify a seller by consulting praise that the seller helped arrange. The dinner extends that problem from online signals into a physical room.
- **Promised reward:** Uneasy recognition plus a useful question about who actually experienced the thing being recommended. Dry absurdity carries the entertainment.
- **Mechanism / limit:** Fabricated reviews helped a real platform ranking; genuine inquiries followed; refusals and actors added signals. The case does not isolate psychological causes or prove all diners were persuaded.
- **Commercial adjacency:** Further sourced cases and interactive credibility practice could deepen this skill. That supports an account learning proposition, not a claim that an offer exists or that viewers will purchase it.

- **Deliberately unused tools:** Fabricated dialogue, character interior monologues, lore, several competing analogies, mandatory five-beat template, hook variants inserted into one script and a sales CTA. They are optional and would weaken this case.
- **Visual material:** Only scene concepts and asset needs are proposed; detailed shots, generated assets and final styling remain behind founder approval.

## Final five questions
1. **Why stop?** A familiar check-and-call decision turns out to involve a shed, and the supposed independent praise was arranged.
2. **What happens / why care?** A person trying to verify quality can still encounter coordinated signals. The birthday inquiry gives the choice an ordinary social stake.
3. **What changes?** Invented reputation attracts actual people; then a physical dinner introduces actual experience and mixed responses.
4. **Does the ending repay the hook?** The viewer learns why the initial checks can share an origin and receives one question about firsthand evidence. No guarantee of detecting deception.
5. **What else can go?** The foot-photo detail is an optional cut if VO needs it, at the cost of one joke. The free-meal and mixed-response qualifications should stay.

## Remaining weaknesses and gates
The opening is a hypothetical second-person reconstruction, not an actual caller transcript. The final source-checking question is a starting habit, not a comprehensive method. The evidence qualifications slow the ending slightly; their accuracy value justifies retaining them. The dinner relies heavily on the organizer's account. The narration still needs a spoken performance to judge rhythm.
